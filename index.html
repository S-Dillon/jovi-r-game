    How to Play

    1

    Learn

    Every lesson starts with a short tutorial. Press ▶ Run on any example to see what it does.

    2

    Experiment

    Write code in the editor and press ▶ Run (or Ctrl+Enter) as often as you like. Nothing is marked until you submit.

    3

    Submit

    When you think you've solved the challenge, press ✔ Submit (Ctrl+Shift+Enter). The game runs your code and checks it.

    4

    Earn XP

    Each challenge is worth XP. Hints are free to look at but cost a little XP. Finish every lesson to clear the level and unlock the next.

    Reading the console

    Lines starting with > are your code. Lines like [1] 42 are R's answers – the [1] just means "first item". Red text is an error: read it carefully, it usually tells you exactly what went wrong, and the 💡 tip underneath will help.
